What Financial Literacy Hub Is

Financial Literacy Hub is a resource built for young adults — particularly students and recent graduates — who want to take control of their money but aren't sure where to start. The site covers the fundamentals: budgeting, saving, understanding credit, and early investing concepts, explained in plain language without the jargon.

Beyond articles, the site offers practical tools: a free budget calculator, a searchable glossary of financial terms, and a product review form so the community can share feedback on the tools they've used.

Who It's For

This site is built around the real questions a first-time visitor is likely searching for — the kind of practical, specific concerns that come up right after a first paycheck, a first credit card, or a first attempt at saving:

  • "I just got my first paycheck — how do I make a budget that actually sticks?"
  • "What does a credit score actually mean, and how do I start building good credit while I'm still in school?"
  • "I've saved a small amount of money — is it too early to start investing?"

Design

The visual design is built around trust and clarity — the two things a financial site needs most. Deep navy grounds the design with authority and seriousness, golden amber adds warmth without losing professionalism, and a warm off-white background keeps longer reading sessions comfortable.
